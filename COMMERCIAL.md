# Support cemainou

cemainou is free for any team, with any number of people, collaborators and clients (see LICENSE.md). Nothing in it waits for a payment: every
feature and every new version comes to everyone.

It is built and kept up by RT Design Studio SRL. If it helps your team, support it: that is how it keeps getting better.

## What supporters get

- A supporter key for your installation (Settings → Support cemainou): the app shows you as a supporter. It unlocks nothing; everything is
  already there.
- Priority support by email, on working days.
- A say in what comes next: when we gather the ideas for new features, supporters get the list first and tell us by email what they want.
- Your company listed among the supporters on rt.md/cemainou, if you want.

## You choose the amount

Every level gives the same. Pick the one that matches what cemainou is worth to your team:

| Level      | Per installation, per year |
|------------|----------------------------|
| Supporter  | €200                       |
| Fair       | €400 (suggested for teams of more than 5 people) |
| Generous   | €900 or more               |

Prices exclude VAT, where it applies. To support: send the installation number (Settings → Support cemainou) to cmn@rt.md with the level you
chose. RT Design Studio SRL sends an invoice, paid by bank transfer; the key comes by email once it is paid. When the year ends, nothing changes
in the app: renew whenever you want.
