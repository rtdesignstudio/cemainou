# What's new in cemainou

The important changes, newest first. Small improvements and fixes come in every version and are not listed one by one.

## 0.1.412 - 2026-10-29

**Outside access, watched.** cemainou keeps a log of what clients and collaborators open: pages, files and what their agents read. Each time, and before anything is sent to them (notification, email, Telegram, weekly digest, monthly report), a second check written apart from the access rules makes sure they are allowed to see it. If not, the admin learns at once, by email and in the bell, and the message does not leave. Someone trying many pages that do not exist is reported too. Everything is under Admin → Outside access.

## 0.1.389 - 2026-10-26

**Office music.** A YouTube jukebox for the team, with no account or key: anyone pastes a link and it comes next, it plays on one computer (the admin chooses who may play it), and everyone sees what plays from the note in the top bar. The player lives in a black panel next to the page and keeps playing while you move around the app. One library for everything the team played: drag it into order, like songs, filter by person or favorites and play them all; after the queue, the whole library plays. Shuffle, repeat (song, queue, everything), volume and a timeline you can jump on; agents can add a link and tell what plays.

## 0.1.387 - 2026-10-25

**Faster pages.** Less work on every page: comments are counted at once and loaded when you open them, the phone menu is drawn only on phones, and Today shows its first blocks first, the rest right after. A thin black bar shows a page loading. All from the code, so it is just as fast on ordinary shared hosting.

## 0.1.385 - 2026-10-25

**Easier on the phone.** Bigger icons in the header and close buttons easy to hit; on a project, the buttons at the top sit in an even grid and the tabs (Overview, Tasks, Ideas, Updates…) scroll sideways, with the active one marked.

## 0.1.384 - 2026-10-25

**Your agent draws it.** Ask for the team's week, a project's timeline or a person's day and the agent draws the chart or the table itself, in black and white, from cemainou's data. It can read any day of a person, hour by hour and by project, when you are allowed to see their hours.

## 0.1.374 - 2026-10-25

**A joke on Today.** Next to the title, a short joke about life outside work, the same for the whole team; anyone can add their own (up to 160 characters), shown with their initials. One click for another, × hides it, and the admin can turn it off.

## 0.1.347 - 2026-10-23

**Free for any team.** cemainou is now free for any team, with any number of people: every feature and every new version, nothing waits for a payment. The license is the FSL, unmodified: only a competing product is not allowed. If it helps you, support it (your choice: €200, €400 or €900 a year) and get a supporter key, priority help and a vote on what comes next. Hosted by RT on aihost.md is coming soon.

## 0.1.346 - 2026-10-23

**Badges for good habits.** Small rewards for what makes a team work well: reporting regularly, unblocking colleagues, noting decisions, closing tasks with a proof, and taking breaks. Never for long hours, and no ranking between people. A new one arrives full screen, like a stamp; your profile shows how close you are to the next tier, and you choose whether the team sees yours. Mark your time off and your streak does not break.

## 0.1.341 - 2026-10-22

**Connecting the agent, in one step.** One question (on the computer, in a chat or another MCP client) and one command for the whole computer: it finds Claude Code, the Claude desktop app, Cursor, Codex and Gemini CLI and connects them all. Paste it in the agent's chat and it runs it; three ticks turn green by themselves: the key, the first session and the tools.

## 0.1.338 - 2026-10-22

**Type @ and pick the person.** In comments, notes and task details, “@” opens the list of people who can be mentioned there: only those who can see the task, the update or the project. Pick with the arrow keys or a click, and they get notified.

## 0.1.329 - 2026-10-22

**A person's page tells the story.** The day is a journal by the hour: updates, tasks, documents and notes in the order they happened, and the time per project is the legend of the hourly bars. At the top you see open, late and blocked tasks at a glance, and the period's figures compare with the period before.

## 0.1.326 - 2026-10-21

**People, and who sees whom.** People is now cards like Today, for the team and collaborators, and each person's page has a period and tabs for their updates, tasks created and done, projects and time. One rule decides who sees whom everywhere: collaborators only see the people of their projects, clients never see other clients.

## 0.1.321 - 2026-10-21

**Correct your time, and a calmer look.** If the measured time of a day is wrong, say how long you really worked and why: the measurement stays, the correction is shown and the totals use it. New square icons everywhere, filters you can see at a glance on Updates, Projects and Hours, and a simpler menu on the phone.

## 0.1.315 - 2026-10-21

**Try it with the whole team first.** A team of any size may use an installation with everyone for up to 30 days before buying a license. Over 5 people without a key, nothing stops: only new versions wait. The license text is now on rt.md/cemainou/license.

## 0.1.309 - 2026-10-21

**Nothing leaves your server unless you choose.** cemainou only checks the list of versions on rt.md. An optional "Keep me posted" in Settings lets us tell you about new versions and help you faster; feedback to RT goes only if you turn it on.

## 0.1.301 - 2026-10-21

**Feedback invitations.** One link with its QR, for a day, a week, a month or until you stop it: the client and their colleagues point at the site and write, each with their name. You see every invitation and stop it with one click.

## 0.1.297 - 2026-10-21

**Search on the whole screen.** Ctrl+K (⌘K) or a tap on Search: a large field, results grouped by kind with the matching words in bold, filters, recent searches and quick actions. "#179" opens the task.

## 0.1.292 - 2026-10-20

**Tasks you see at a glance.** Bigger titles, overdue and due-today tasks that stand out, drag and drop between groups, project stages with the time spent on each, and a connection check in the profile that says what is missing.

## 0.1.283 - 2026-10-20

**The team keeps moving.** Anyone on the project can take over a task, close it or hand it to someone else when the lead forgot; the person who had it is told.

## 0.1.280 - 2026-10-20

**Time from the past, on the project.** Days rebuilt from git or from file dates get their estimated time, and the next /upd fills in the days that had none. Estimates stay apart from measured hours.

## 0.1.263 - 2026-10-20

**Free for small teams, one price per installation.** Up to five people, cemainou is free. Above that, one license per installation, with any number of people, brings a year of new versions; what you have keeps working.

## 0.1.257 - 2026-10-20

**whatsupai is now cemainou.** A new name for the same app; existing installations and agents move to it by themselves.

## 0.1.240 - 2026-10-18

**Notifications on the phone and the computer.** New tasks, comments and answers from the client reach you even with cemainou closed.

## 0.1.178 - 2026-10-15

**Email becomes a task.** Every project has its own address: an email sent or forwarded there becomes a task, with its files.

## 0.1.152 - 2026-10-15

**Feedback right on the client's site.** A button on the live site, only for people with an account: point at what to change, and it becomes a task with the screenshot and the page.

## 0.1.61 - 2026-10-13

**My day.** On Today, how long you worked and on which projects, measured from your agent sessions, without a timer.

## 0.1.47 - 2026-10-12

**Connect the Claude and ChatGPT apps.** Ask about your tasks and projects from the phone or the desktop app, signed in with your account.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-08

**The first version, of an idea long in the making.** Your team's AI agents write the updates, open and close tasks and log the time; people read, decide and answer. On your own server.
